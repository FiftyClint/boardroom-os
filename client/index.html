They are offering $4M cash upfront and a 20-year commitment to take 30MW, provided we can guarantee delivery by Q4 next year. The Data Center Operator
First, "provided we guarantee delivery by Q4" is not an offer, it's a catastrophic risk transfer. No one guarantees delivery on a 30MW substation expansion in 18 months in this supply chain environment. Do they have their own long-lead gear secured? Do the transformers even exist on a factory floor anywhere? If you sign that guarantee and miss by 30 days, they use the default clauses to renegotiate the entire deal or walk away with their deposit. Push the delivery risk back to them or tie it specifically to utility interconnection timelines.

Have you considered simply leasing them the land and making them assume all execution risk on the delivery timeframe?
